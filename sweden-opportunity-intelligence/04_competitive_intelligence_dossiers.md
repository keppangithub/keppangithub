**How to read the figures:**
- Financials come from public Swedish filing summaries on allabolag.se and are in SEK, for the latest filing year (mostly 2025).
- Where revenue, pricing or customer counts could not be found, the dossier says **"not found"**. Nothing is estimated.

**Market classification key:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Underserved | Demand exists and no supplier serves it well |
| Fragmented | Many small suppliers |
| Consolidating | Larger players are buying up smaller ones |
| Saturated | Well served already |
| Dominated | One or a few strong incumbents |
| Structurally unattractive | The market's structure works against any new entrant |
| Emerging-unproven | Too early to know whether demand will materialise |

**Customers already spend money:**
- Uppsala advertised a SEK 100m framework (TED 307120-2026).
- Adda's area-7 framework is in place, with direct call-offs up to SEK 150k going to the top-ranked supplier.
- State grants to municipalities were SEK 739m in 2025.

**Implication for an entrant:**
- The national Adda framework is closed until 2030.
- Routes in are:
  - municipal direct awards under the direktupphandling threshold;
  - regional and municipal frameworks (Uppsala-type);
  - subcontracting to framework holders;
  - municipal companies and private säkerhetskänslig verksamhet, which procure separately.

## Where customers already pay for inadequate workarounds

1. Municipalities and municipal companies pay **hourly consultant rates of SEK 1,000–1,700** for security work (ITK price appendix, W07) and run säkerhetsskydd with single-person teams.
2. Accounting firms pay **per-seat plus per-client fees** to several ledger vendors and complain about it (W09 §1; W05 C6).
3. BRF boards pay for **separate** apps, maintenance plans, OVK firms and energy experts (W08 §1–2).
4. Small municipalities pay neighbours or consultants **SEK 0.45–1.4m per year** for procurement capacity (W07 Area 5).

An absence of competitors was treated as a warning sign, not a positive. That applied to defence-SME onboarding (thin market) and the CRA PSIRT service (demand not yet proven).
