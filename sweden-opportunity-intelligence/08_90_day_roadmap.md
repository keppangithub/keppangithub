# Deliverable 8: 90-day founder roadmap: Swedish NIS2 operations partner

**Assumed start:** Monday 2 November 2026. **Team:** two founders, at least one with security or IT credibility.

**90-day budget (cash, excluding founder salaries):**

| Item | SEK |
|---|---|
| Company formation (aktiebolag), with share capital of 25,000 kept in the company | 27,000 |
| Professional indemnity + cyber liability insurance (quote needed; assumption) | 15,000 |
| Tooling (M365, CRM, hosting, domain, e-signing) | 12,000 |
| ISO 27001 Lead Implementer training/certification for one founder (assumption) | 25,000 |
| Travel for on-site briefings | 20,000 |
| Webinar/event costs, content design | 10,000 |
| Legal (standard terms, data-processing agreement, scope letter templates) | 25,000 |
| Contingency | 16,000 |
| **Total** | **~150,000** |

If the founders draw salaries, add about SEK 59k per founder per month fully loaded (employer contributions 31.42% included). That is about SEK 355k for two founders over 90 days.

**Rules:**
- No customer contact misrepresents the company or implies endorsements.
- **Expressions of interest and letters of intent are logged but never counted as revenue.**
- Only signed orders or paid invoices count.

## Days 1–14: Problem validation

| Week | Milestones | Measurable output |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** (2–8 Nov) | 1. Register the AB, bank account and insurance quotes. 2. Build the prospect list: municipal companies in SNI 35/36/37/38 (SCB/Bolagsverket) + private Annex I/II entities with ≥50 employees; filter for scope (water ≥20,000 persons served). 3. Draft the interview guide: current status, who owns NIS2, budget source, existing providers, pain, procurement route. | ≥250 records in CRM, ≥120 qualified as in scope; interview guide v1 |
| **W2** (9–15 Nov) | 1. 20 discovery calls with IT managers, säkerhetschefer and CEOs (no selling). 2. Collect evidence of **existing spending**: what they pay consultants, MSP contract scope, whether KLASSA is used. 3. Ask 3 municipal procurement officers how they would buy a SEK 60k gap analysis and a 2-year retainer under the SEK 700k direct-award limit. | ≥15 completed interviews; spend data from ≥8; procurement route confirmed by ≥2 buyers |

**Gate 1 (day 14):**
- **Continue** if at least 8 of 15 interviewees report an unresolved NIS2 gap **and** at least 5 report budget or spend in that area.
- **Stop or rethink** if more than half say "our MSP/KLASSA covers it".

## Days 15–30: Commercial validation

| Week | Milestones | Measurable output |
|---|---|---|
| **W3** (16–22 Nov) | 1. Finalise the offer: gap analysis SEK 60k (pilot SEK 40k for the first 3 reference clients); implementation SEK 90k; retainer SEK 18k/month. 2. Standard terms, scope letter, data-processing agreement. 3. One-page offer plus a landing page with a "book a board briefing" call to action. 4. Outbound sequence to the 120 qualified prospects. | Offer live; ≥60 prospects contacted |
| **W4** (23–29 Nov) | 1. Run 8–10 free board briefings (45 min). 2. Host one webinar with a regional association ("Cybersäkerhetslagen in practice: personal liability and 24h reporting"). 3. Ask for the order at the end of each briefing. | ≥8 briefings; ≥2 **signed** gap-analysis orders; objections logged |

**Gate 2 (day 30):**
- **Continue** with at least 2 signed paid orders (POs or signed agreements).
- **Pivot to MSP partnering** with exactly 1 order or a consistent "MSP covers it" objection.
- **Stop** with 0 orders and fewer than 5 briefings.

## Days 31–60: Build the minimum viable product (only what the paid pilots need)

| Week | Milestones | Measurable output |
|---|---|---|
| **W5** (30 Nov–6 Dec) | Build the control library v1 (SFS 2025:1506, förordning 2025:1507, MCFFS 2026:1, Impl. Reg. 2024/2690); gap-analysis questionnaire and scoring sheet | Library ≥90 controls mapped |
| **W6** (7–13 Dec) | Deliver gap analysis #1 on site; time-track every hour | Report delivered; hours logged |
| **W7** (14–20 Dec) | Policy and runbook template pack; incident-reporting checklist with 24h/72h/1-month timers; management-training deck and quiz | Pack v1 used in a client deliverable |
| **W8** (21 Dec–3 Jan, holidays) | Light week: internal ISO 27001-aligned controls (MFA, access, logging, data segregation); hosting decision (EU/Swedish); begin the Microsoft Graph/Entra read-only evidence script | Internal security checklist complete |

**Delivery and security safeguards:**
- Client data is stored only in segregated workspaces, and no client credentials are stored.
- Signed data-processing agreements.
- Professional indemnity insurance is bound before the first delivery.
- Every recommendation in a report is reviewed by a second person.

**Gate 3 (day 60):**
- Gap analyses must be delivered at ≤40 hours each. That is the margin check: SEK 60k ÷ 40h = SEK 1,500/h effective.
- At least 1 client must have agreed to discuss implementation or a retainer.

## Day-90 decision criteria

| Metric | GO (continue and plan the first hire) | PIVOT | STOP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid orders (signed and invoiced) | ≥4 gap analyses **and** ≥1 retainer or implementation | 2–3 gap analyses, 0 retainers | ≤1 gap analysis |
| Cash collected | ≥SEK 150k | SEK 60–150k | <SEK 60k |
| Pipeline (briefings held) | ≥20 | 10–19 | <10 |
| Conversion (briefing → paid) | ≥20% | 10–19% | <10% |
| Delivery effort per gap analysis | ≤40 h | 40–60 h | >60 h (re-price or stop) |
| Dominant objection | Price or timing (solvable) | "MSP covers it" → MSP channel pivot | "Not needed / no budget" in >50% |
| Client outcome | Board trained and runbook tested at ≥1 client | — | Clients dissatisfied |

**Pivot options:**
1. White-label delivery through MSPs.
2. Focus on private mid-size entities only (faster cycles).
3. Bring forward CRA PSIRT validation for manufacturers.
