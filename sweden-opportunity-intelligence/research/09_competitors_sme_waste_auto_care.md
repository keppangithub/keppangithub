## 1. Workflow / automation / AI layer for small redovisningsbyråer

### Market context
- Industry growth was 1.6% in 2025, below inflation. EBITDA margin rose from 5.1% to 7.3%. Staff costs are about 57% of revenue. Firms with staff costs under 55% often reach more than 10% EBITDA; firms over 70% are often negative. Source: Oxceed, 171 firms [S15].
- Björn Lundén and Revisionsvärlden surveyed more than 400 byråer (May 2026). Byråer say their own clients are holding back automation [S6].
- Fortnox says more than 10,000 accounting firms use it [S2]. EQT's take-private of Fortnox (about SEK 55 bn) is cited in the Spiris industry report [S16].
- **Fortnox Access** is a new mandatory per-client fee. Tiers depend on vouchers in the last 12 months: 0 = 9 kr, 1/40–99 = 29 kr, 100–999 = 99 kr, 1,000+ = 199 kr per month.
  - New clients pay from 1 May 2026.
  - Existing clients where the byrå is sole payer or administrator are billed from the start of a new contract period from 1 Oct 2026 [S3][S4].
  - Byråer criticised it as compulsory [S5].
- In autumn 2025 the byrå licence was raised by about 200 kr per byråkonsult per month after more programs were bundled in (search snippet of Affärsvärlden; paywalled) [S5].

## 3. Municipal packaging collection (FNI from 1 Jan 2027): operations and compensation-reporting software

**Regulation:**
- Every municipality must run fastighetsnära insamling (FNI) of paper, plastic, metal and glass packaging by 1 Jan 2027 [S24].
- Naturvårdsverket decides compensation based on quarterly reports. Municipalities report within 3 weeks after quarter end, and decisions cannot be appealed. Kammarkollegiet pays out. Q1 2026 was decided on 16 Jun 2026, with payout around week 30 [S24][S25].
- About 75 municipalities had introduced FNI per Avfall Sverige (TV4, date unclear) [S26].
- A draft revision (NV-26-072914) for 2027 adds compensation for transshipment and handover and a new fixed compensation for easily accessible collection points. Material-differentiated compensation stays for 2027–28, with a new model from 2029 [S24].

**2026 compensation levels** (indexed, published 2026-02-17) [S27]:
- Fixed per connected household per year: for example group 1 single-family homes, paper 287 + plastic 263 + glass 95 + metal 72 = **717 kr/household/yr**; apartments 120 + 131 + 48 + 36 = **335 kr**. Group 8 single-family homes: 478 + 418 + 167 + 108 = **1,171 kr**.
- Sparsity top-up of up to 156 kr per material.
- Per tonne: paper 1,793, plastic 1,554, metal 371, glass 669 kr.
- Easily accessible collection point and ÅVC: 95,700–167,400 kr/yr each.
- Information: 50,000 kr/yr for municipalities under 10k inhabitants, 5 kr per inhabitant above that.

This is a household-count-driven, multi-category, quarterly claim. That makes data quality (connected households per category) directly worth money.

- Of the Sörab municipalities, 6 of 9 have their own system [S30].

**Classification:** **Dominated** at the core-system layer, with an active **re-procurement wave** (Avfall Sverige project, Täby, Upplands Väsby, Sysav). The compensation-reporting and reconciliation niche is **underserved**, but buyers are public bodies under LOU, with 10-year contracts and long sales cycles. That makes it a **structurally slow** market.
