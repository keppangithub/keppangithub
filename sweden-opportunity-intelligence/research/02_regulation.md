# 02 — Regulatory demand drivers in Sweden, 2026–2030

**Reference date:** 29 October 2026. **Scope:** Swedish and EU rules that create recurring or one-off business demand, or act as barriers, in Sweden.
**Method:** Web search and fetch of official sources (riksdagen.se, regeringen.se, lagen.nu/SFS, EUR-Lex references, Boverket, Naturvårdsverket, Migrationsverket, MCF, PTS) plus law-firm and trade-body secondary sources. Each claim carries a status tag:

- **[V]** verified against an official or primary text in this session.
- **[S]** secondary sources only, though several agree.
- **[U]** not verified in this session. These come from background knowledge or a single weak source, and must be re-checked before use.

> **Research limitation:** the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. DORA, the bokföringslag 2024 changes, instant payments, PSD3/PSR, the Late Payment Regulation, the whistleblower law, DOS-lagen, OVK/radon and the final Swedish AI Act and Data Act laws could not be re-verified in this session. They are tagged [U].

> **Political context (important):** the Riksdag election was held on **13 Sept 2026**. Secondary reports say the opposition bloc won narrowly (about 176 seats to 173). On 15 Oct the Riksdag voted down the Speaker's first PM candidate, Magdalena Andersson. On about 19 Oct government formation was still under way [S]. Bills tabled before the election stay with the Riksdag, but a new government can withdraw or reprioritise them. This matters for prop. 2025/26:303 (CER), prop. 2025/26:282 (personalliggare/ROT-RUT), Pay Transparency, the platform work law, the Swedish AI Act supplementary law, the Data Act supplementary law, and the e-invoicing mandate.

## 2. Per-regulation detail

### 2.1 NIS2 → Cybersäkerhetslag (2025:1506)
- **Status:** SFS 2025:1506 (law) and SFS 2025:1507 (förordning) are **in force from 15 Jan 2026** and replace lag (2018:1174) [V, lagen.nu/riksdagen]. Sweden was about 15 months late on the 17 Oct 2024 EU deadline. MSB became **Myndigheten för civilt försvar (MCF)** on 1 Jan 2026 [S]. MCF issued **MCFFS 2026:1** on notification and identification (decided 8 Jan 2026) [V/S].
- **Scope:** Medium and large entities in Annex I/II sectors; selected small entities (sole provider, regionally important, trust services); public e-comms; digital providers with main establishment in Sweden (cloud, data centre, CDN, MSP, marketplaces, DNS). **Public sector: all regions, municipalities and municipal associations**, plus designated state agencies. DORA entities and säkerhetsskydd activities are carved out [V].
- **Count:** About **5,000–8,000** entities (Two Birds; MSB 2025 about 5,000), against about 900 under NIS1 [S]. No official MCF registration count was found.
- **Duties:** Register "as soon as possible" and report changes within 14 days. Risk-management measures (risk analysis, incident handling, BCM, supply chain, cyber hygiene, crypto, access control). **Mandatory management training.** Incident reporting: early warning within 24h, notification within 72h (24h for trust services), final report within 1 month [V]. Reporting goes to the CSIRT function at FRA/NCSC (moved from MCF; single-source claim that this happened 1 Jul 2026 [U]).
- **Supervision:** Sector supervisors designated by the government. Examples: Transportstyrelsen (transport), PTS (digital infrastructure), IVO/health, FI-adjacent, länsstyrelser, Energimarknadsinspektionen (energy) [S].
- **Sanctions:** Essential entities up to the higher of **2% of global turnover or EUR 10m**. Important entities up to the higher of **1.4% or EUR 7m**. Public bodies up to **SEK 10m**. Management ban of 1–3 years for essential entities [V].
- **Vendors in Sweden:** Truesec, Sectra (won a national SOC assignment [S]), Combitech, Knowit, Netsol, Advania, Atea, Tieto, Basalt, Omegapoint, Secify, Big4, Two Birds, Mannheimer Swartling, Setterwalls. Many have NIS2 GRC tooling.
- **Opportunity:** Standardised **"NIS2-in-a-box" for municipalities and mid-size important entities**: ISMS templates aligned with MCF guidance, management-training certification, incident-reporting runbooks, supplier-security questionnaires, and managed SOC/MDR priced for 50–500 FTE organisations. Supply-chain pass-through matters too: suppliers to the 5–8k entities face contractual security demands even when out of scope.
- **Demand profile:** One-off gap remediation in 2026–27, then **recurring** audits, training, MDR and annual reviews. **Strongest recurring driver in the list.**

### 2.2 Cyber Resilience Act (EU 2024/2847)
- **Status:** Vulnerability and incident reporting under **Art. 14 applies from 11 Sep 2026** through ENISA's **Single Reporting Platform**, reported live on that day [S]. Early warning for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents within 24h. Commission guidance published 27 Jul 2026 [S]. Full obligations (essential requirements, conformity assessment, CE marking) apply **11 Dec 2027**; notified-body provisions apply earlier (Jun 2026) [U].
- **Sanctions:** Up to EUR 15m or 2.5% of turnover [S].
- **Affected:** All makers, importers and distributors of hardware and software with digital elements sold in the EU: Swedish industrial automation, connected consumer products, embedded and SaaS-with-client software. Open-source stewards have lighter rules.
- **Opportunity:** PSIRT-as-a-service, SBOM tooling and integration, product threat modelling, CRA conformity readiness for SME manufacturers, and support with harmonised standards. The deadline is hard (Dec 2027), so 2027 demand is **high**. After that, recurring vulnerability handling is required for the support period (at least 5 years).

### 2.3 AI Act + Digital Omnibus on AI
- **Status:** **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** amends 2024/1689. EP vote 16 Jun, Council 29 Jun, published 24 Jul, **in force 27 Jul 2026** [S, Hunton, A&O, Mondaq, NI Assembly EU-acts listing].
- **Timeline now:** Prohibitions and AI literacy (Art. 4) since **2 Feb 2025**. GPAI since **2 Aug 2025**. Art. 50 transparency from **2 Aug 2026**. Marking of AI-generated content for systems already on the market by **2 Dec 2026**. New prohibition on AI generating CSAM or non-consensual intimate imagery from **2 Dec 2026**. **Annex III stand-alone high-risk (HR/recruitment, education, credit scoring, essential public services) from 2 Dec 2027**. Annex I product-embedded systems from **2 Aug 2028** [S].
- **AI literacy:** The omnibus changed the Art. 4 wording. The Commission's proposal turned it into an obligation on Member States and the Commission to "promote" literacy, but the final wording was **not verified** [U]. Do not sell AI-literacy courses as a hard legal mandate without checking the OJ text.
- **SME relief:** Simplified documentation extended to small mid-caps. Simplified registration for systems a provider deems not high-risk [S].
- **Swedish supplementary law:** SOU 2025:101 proposed **PTS** as the main market-surveillance authority, with IMY and FI for parts of Annex III, entering into force 2 Aug 2026. PTS is reportedly merging with **Digg** [S]. **No enacted SFS or proposition was found in this session** [U]. Sweden is likely late on designating authorities and setting penalties.
- **Opportunity:** AI inventories and governance for municipalities and regions (which use AI in social services and HR). HR-tech compliance for Annex III recruitment tools before Dec 2027. Deepfake labelling and watermarking for media and marketing (Aug/Dec 2026). **Most current demand is voluntary governance and training. The hard spend arrives late 2027–2028.**

### 2.4 Accessibility: EAA (Lag 2023:254) and DOS-lagen
- **EAA:** **In force 28 Jun 2025** [S]. Covers e-commerce, consumer banking, e-books, passenger-transport ticketing and info, e-comms, and certain hardware (ATMs, ticket machines, terminals, PCs, phones). Microenterprises are exempt for services. **PTS** supervises e-commerce and other services. Konsumentverket and others cover products [S]. Sanction fee reported **up to SEK 10m** (§37) [U, secondary]. PTS supervision of e-commerce is stepwise through 2025–26. No published e-commerce sanction case was found.
- **DOS-lagen (2018:1937):** Public-sector web and app accessibility, supervised by Digg (merging with PTS) [U].
- **Vendors:** Funka, Axess Lab, Siteimprove, Monsido/Acquia, Useit, Knowit, Netlight. AI-based remediation overlays are controversial.
- **Opportunity:** Recurring audits plus remediation retainers for mid-size e-tailers and banks. Accessibility in procurement (EN 301 549) for public buyers. **Moderate recurring spend.** Enforcement is light so far, which caps urgency.

### 2.5 CSRD / ESRS / Taxonomy / VSME / CSDDD after Omnibus I
- **EU:** Directive (EU) **2026/470** was published in the OJ on 26 Feb 2026 and is in force **18 Mar 2026**. CSRD scope is now **>1,000 employees AND >EUR 450m net turnover**. CSRD transposition is due **19 Mar 2027**. CSDDD scope is **>5,000 employees and >EUR 1.5bn turnover**, with transposition by **26 Jul 2028**. The **climate transition plan duty in CSDDD was removed** [S, multiple law firms]. Estimated EU scope is about 5,000 companies, down from about 50,000 [S].
- **Sweden:** The "stop-the-clock" law (prop. 2025/26:21, bet. 2025/26:CU8) postponed wave 2 and wave 3 reporting by two years [S]. Wave 1 large PIEs (>500 employees) continue reporting under ÅRL transitional rules. **No Swedish bill transposing 2026/470 was found.** Finland transposed it in Jun 2026 (555/2026) [S]. A Swedish bill is expected in 2026–27 [U].
- **VSME:** Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1710. A draft delegated act for the **value-chain cap** (based on VSME, 2 modules, about 20 disclosures) was published 6 May 2026 [S]. It protects companies under 1,000 employees from data requests beyond the cap.
- **Taxonomy:** A Taxonomy simplification delegated act (materiality threshold, reduced templates) is reported adopted in 2025–26 [U]. Not verified this session.
- **Opportunity assessment:** **Demand has collapsed for mid-caps.** Remaining demand: (a) about 100–300 large Swedish groups doing ESRS and limited assurance (Big4 dominated); (b) **VSME-lite reporting for SMEs, driven by banks (green loans, EBA ESG risk) and large customers**. This is low-ticket and SaaS-friendly (e.g. Position Green, Normative, Greenly, Worldfavor, Sweep, Coolset). Treat any "CSRD for SMEs" pitch as **hype**.

### 2.6 EPBD recast and Swedish energideklarationer
- **Status:** Prop. **2025/26:159** was adopted. Amendments to **PBL** and **lagen (2006:985) om energideklaration för byggnader** are **in force 1 Jul 2026** [V regeringen; S Boverket]. Implementation was slightly late against the 29 May 2026 deadline.
- **Content [V]:** Energy declarations are required after **renovations affecting a significant part of the climate envelope**. **All buildings owned or used by public bodies** must have a declaration. A simplified update option is introduced. **Inspections of heating, ventilation and AC systems at 3- or 5-year intervals** depending on size. **Voluntary energy renovation plans** (Sweden's light-touch renovation passport). Building owners must provide information for supervision. PBL charging requirement becomes "sustainable mobility" (cycle parking).
- **Boverket:** New energy class scale with **A0** from 25 May 2026 [S]. Nine draft regulations were consulted on in Feb 2026, with final rules planned **1 Oct 2026** [S]. Solar-installation requirements on new and renovated public and non-residential buildings come from EPBD Art. 10 (phased from end-2026) [U]; Swedish treatment not confirmed. National building renovation plan due 31 Dec 2026.
- **Who:** About 2.5m buildings in Sweden [U]. Public-sector building stock (municipalities, regions, state property agencies, allmännyttan) is directly hit.
- **Vendors:** Certified energy experts (about 400–600 active in Sweden [U]), Sweco, WSP, Ramboll, AFRY, Bengt Dahlgren, Energieffektivisering i Sverige AB, local firms. Software: Bidcon, Vitec Energy, Enviro.
- **Opportunity:** **HVAC inspections plus extra declaration triggers mean real recurring volume.** Bundling energy declaration, OVK (mandatory ventilation inspection, pre-existing), radon measurement, renovation plan and solar feasibility into one subscription for BRFs and municipal portfolios is a plausible new-entrant play.

### 2.7 Building rules, PBL and klimatdeklaration
- Boverket's **new building regulations** (BFS 2024:6–13, nine parts mirroring PBL/PBF) replaced BBR/EKS from **1 Jul 2025**, with the transition ending **30 Jun 2026** [S]. This was one-off retraining demand and is now absorbed.
- **Klimatdeklaration** has applied since 2022 for new buildings (construction stage). **Limit values** proposed by Boverket from 2027 and an extended declaration (more life-cycle stages) went out for consultation. **No adopted bill was found** [S]. EPBD requires a whole-life-carbon roadmap by 1 Jan 2027 and whole-life GWP disclosure for new buildings over 1,000 m² from 2028 and all new buildings from 2030 [U].
- **Opportunity:** LCA and EPD consultancy, generic climate data tools (Boverket's database, One Click LCA). Growing but tied to construction volumes, which are currently depressed.
- **OVK and radon:** Long-standing recurring inspections, a mature market [U].

### 2.8 PPWR and Swedish packaging producer responsibility
- **PPWR (EU 2025/40):** In force 11 Feb 2025, **applies 12 Aug 2026** [S, Naturvårdsverket]. Later milestones include labelling (about 2028), recyclability grades (2030), PFAS limits in food packaging (Aug 2026), reuse targets (2030) and recycled-content targets (2030) [U for exact dates].
- **Sweden:** Förordning (2022:1274) was amended (reportedly SFS 2026:1462 [U]) to adopt the PPWR **producer definition from 12 Aug 2026**. Producers must register with Naturvårdsverket and report annually by **31 March**. Two approved PROs: **Näringslivets Producentansvar (NPA)** and **TMR** [S]. Supplementary national rules are still being drafted [S].
- **Municipal collection:** From **1 Jan 2026** municipalities collect packaging in squares and parks. By **1 Jan 2027** all municipalities must provide **property-close collection (fastighetsnära insamling)** of paper, plastic, metal and glass from households (SFS 2025:825; dispensations possible). Producers compensate municipalities via Naturvårdsverket regulations [S].
- **Opportunity:** (a) **EPR data and reporting SaaS** for small importers and e-tailers, many of whom became "producers" under the new definition. This is recurring and low-ticket but high-count (tens of thousands of firms [U]). (b) **Municipal collection logistics**: bins, multi-compartment vehicles, procurement advice, route optimisation. A one-off 2026–27 surge, then recurring service contracts. (c) Packaging redesign and recyclability assessment (2027–2030).

### 2.9 Textile EPR
Directive (EU) **2025/1892** (WFD revision) has been in force since 16 Oct 2025. Transposition is due **17 Jun 2027**. EPR schemes must be operational about **Apr 2028**, with micro-enterprises a year later [S]. Sweden's earlier national textile EPR proposal was shelved. No Swedish transposition text was found. **Demand from 2027–28:** PRO formation, fee modulation data, and sorting and recycling capacity (Sysav, Siptex, Renewcell's successor).

### 2.10 Battery Regulation
In force. **Due diligence postponed to 18 Aug 2027** (stop-the-clock, OJ 30 Jul 2025), with a proposal to exempt companies under EUR 150m turnover [S]. The **battery passport** (EV, LMT, industrial batteries >2 kWh) applies **18 Feb 2027** per the original Art. 77 [U, not re-verified; check for any Omnibus IV/VIII change]. Carbon-footprint declarations are phased. This is a niche demand pool (Volvo, Polestar, Scania, Epiroc, Nilar, battery importers). DPP data platforms (Circularise, Minespider, Siemens) are already active.

### 2.11 ESPR / DPP
The DPP registry was set up by **Implementing Reg. (EU) 2026/1778** and reportedly went live about 20 Jul 2026, with no registration obligation yet [S]. **No product-group delegated act had been adopted** by Oct 2026. Steel is expected late 2026 and textiles 2027, each with at least 18 months transition, so **the first real obligations come around 2028–2029** [S]. A ban on destroying unsold apparel and footwear applies to large companies from **19 Jul 2026** [U]. **Near-term DPP demand is hype**, except for batteries.

### 2.12 EUDR
After two delays, the Commission review (**COM(2026) 191, 4 May 2026**) **ruled out further postponement or reopening**. It published simplified guidance and a draft delegated act on scope, and claims about 75% lower costs [S]. **Application: 30 Dec 2026** for large and medium operators, **30 Jun 2027** for micro and small. An amended Annex I reportedly entered into force 18 Sep 2026, with new products from 30 Dec 2027 [S, single source]. **The Swedish forest industry (wood, pulp, paper, furniture, packaging) is directly exposed.** Skogsstyrelsen is the competent authority for timber [U]. Opportunity: geolocation and due-diligence data services for forest owners' associations (Södra, Mellanskog, Norra), sawmills and importers. **Concentrated but real near-term demand.**

### 2.13 Financial sector: DORA, AMLR, instant payments, PSD3
- **DORA:** Applies since 17 Jan 2025. Registers of information, major-incident reporting, TLPT, CTPP oversight by the ESAs [U]. Drives ICT contract re-papering and third-party risk management. Swedish vendors: Big4, Advisense, Nexer, Truesec, Netrisk.
- **AMLR (EU 2024/1624):** Applies **10 Jul 2027**. AMLD6 transposition due 10 Jul 2027. AMLA is operational in Frankfurt [S]. A Swedish proposal merges the penningtvättslag and the beneficial-owner law into one new law, now in consultation [S]. Obliged entities include small ones such as accounting firms, real-estate agents, art and luxury dealers, and cash-heavy businesses (a EUR 10k cash cap applies EU-wide). **Recurring KYC/screening SaaS demand** (Trapets, Kidbrooke, Roaring, Bisnode/D&B, Creditsafe).
- **Instant Payments (EU 2024/886)** and **PSD3/PSR**: Mostly bank IT. Limited SME service demand [U].

### 2.14 Data Act, GDPR, whistleblower
- **Data Act:** Applies since **12 Sep 2025**. PTS was named competent authority (Oct 2025). The supplementary Swedish law (SOU 2025:118) was proposed for 1 Jul 2026, but **no SFS was found** [S]. Connected-product design obligations apply to products placed on the market from Sep 2026 [U]. Cloud-switching fees end Jan 2027 [U]. Demand: contract templates, data-sharing APIs for industrial IoT makers.
- **GDPR (IMY):** Steady enforcement. 2026 fines include Sportadmin SEK 6m (Jan 2026, breach involving children's data) and Klarna SEK 7.5m (Jul 2026). The Spotify SEK 58m fine was partly reduced on appeal [S]. SFS 2026:1589 amended the Swedish GDPR supplementary act (Aug 2026) [S]. Mature market.
- **Whistleblower (2021:890):** Mature and commoditised (WhistleB/NAVEX, Lantero, Visslan, Trumpet) [U].

### 2.15 Pay Transparency Directive
- **Status [V/S]:** A lagrådsremiss was issued in Jan 2026 (proposed in force 1 Jul 2026, later pushed to 1 Jan 2027). The bill planned for 17 Mar 2026 was **never tabled**. On **26 Mar 2026** the government paused the work and sought EU renegotiation. On **22 May 2026** the Commission refused to renegotiate or delay. The EU deadline of **7 Jun 2026 has passed without transposition**. Almega (updated 7 Oct 2026) expects **no Swedish law in 2026 and probably none in 2027**. Only 5 of 27 member states had transposed by Jun 2026. **Infringement proceedings are likely.** Possible **direct vertical effect for public employers** (KPMG, DO) [S].
- **Note:** The new post-election government may change course, since the S-led opposition was more favourable.
- **Existing Swedish duty:** Diskrimineringslagen already requires an annual pay survey (lönekartläggning) for all employers, written for 10+ employees. A pay-analysis market already exists (Lönekartläggning.se, Sympa, Ravio, Fortnox and Spiris modules).
- **Opportunity:** A deferred wave. Prepare tooling (job architecture, gender pay-gap reporting) and sell to public employers first.

### 2.16 Platform Work Directive
SOU 2026:3 proposes a separate platform-work law. Consultation closed 31 Mar 2026. **No bill was found.** The EU deadline of **2 Dec 2026** will be missed [S]. The affected population is narrow. **Low opportunity.**

### 2.17 Tax and bookkeeping: personalliggare, kassaregister, e-invoicing, bokföring
- **Prop. 2025/26:282** ("Effektivare kontrollmöjligheter i systemen för rot, rut, grön teknik och personalliggare") was tabled 4 Jun 2026 and is **proposed in force 1 Jan 2027** [S]. It builds on SOU 2024:61, which proposed **mandatory electronic personalliggare in all covered industries**. Confirm the final bill text and the Riksdag vote. Covered industries: construction (electronic since 2016, with ID06 the de facto standard), restaurants, hairdressers and beauty, laundries, car washes, wholesale tobacco and food, car repair [U on the latest list]. **Opportunity:** recurring SaaS for small restaurants and salons moving from paper to digital (Caspeco, Personalkollen, Quinyx, ID06 partners).
- **Kassaregister:** Updated technical requirements (**SKVFS 2021:17**) are mandatory from **1 Jan 2027**. Older control units are allowed only until 31 Dec 2026 [S]. Skatteverket's Mar 2026 review considers extending control to all non-invoiced B2C sales [S]. This gives a **short-term POS replacement and upgrade spike**.
- **E-invoicing:** B2G Peppol has been mandatory since 2019, with only Peppol BIS 3.0 accepted from Jul 2025 [S]. The government appointed an inquiry (**Dir. 2026:9**, 5 Feb 2026) on a domestic B2B mandate and ViDA, reporting about Nov 2027 [S]. **ViDA**: digital reporting for intra-EU B2B from **1 Jul 2030**, and member states may mandate domestic e-invoicing without derogation from 2025 [U]. **The earliest realistic Swedish B2B mandate is about 2029–2030.** It will be large when it comes (about 1.2m VAT-registered entities [U]). Now it is positioning only (Peppol access points such as Pagero/Thomson Reuters, InExchange, Crediflow, Fortnox, Visma).
- **Bokföringslag (2024):** Since 1 Jul 2024, paper originals may be destroyed once converted to electronic form, with relaxed rules on archiving abroad [U]. A mature digitisation market.

### 2.18 Machinery Regulation (EU 2023/1230)
It applies **20 Jan 2027** and replaces 2006/42/EC. The Digital Omnibus on AI (2026/1744) also amends it on AI safety components [S]. Omnibus IV (digital declarations of conformity) excluded machinery from the transition period [S]. Member states notified penalties by 20 Oct 2026. Sweden's machine-building base (ABB, Atlas Copco, Sandvik, Epiroc, Tetra Pak, about 2,000+ SME machine builders [U]) needs updated risk assessments, cybersecurity for safety functions, and new conformity routes for six high-risk categories. Vendors: RISE, Intertek Semko, Kiwa, TÜV, Pilz, Sick. **One-off peak in 2026–27, then ongoing.**

### 2.19 Civil defence, CER, säkerhetsskydd, beredskapsplikt
- **Funding:** BP 2026 has a civil-defence package of about **SEK 12bn for 2026–2028 (about SEK 3bn in 2026)**, with about SEK 4.6bn to municipalities and regions [S]. In 2026 **MCF is distributing >SEK 1.5bn** to municipalities: SEK 649m to all municipalities for crisis preparedness and civil defence, SEK 400m for supply-preparedness materiel, and SEK 490m targeted (with the Armed Forces) to selected municipalities for **command centres and "trygghetspunkter"** [S]. About **SEK 3.5bn in 2026–28 for population protection including shelter renovation** (about SEK 1.2bn in 2026) [S]. The civil-defence frame is to rise to **about SEK 15bn/yr from 2028** [S].
- **CER → prop. 2025/26:303:** New law (Lagrådet suggested renaming it "lag om motståndskraft i samhällsviktiga tjänster") **proposed in force 1 Jan 2027** [V regeringen]. It covers critical entities in 11 sectors (energy, transport, banking, health, drinking water, food, digital infrastructure, etc.). Duties: risk assessments, resilience measures, incident reporting, starting 10 months after designation. The bill also **raises sanction fees under the säkerhetsskyddslag for private operators** [V]. Riksdag decision pending. Post-election risk exists but is low, since the policy has cross-bloc support.
- **Säkerhetsskyddslag (SFS 2026:764), in force 1 Jul 2026:** Interim injunctions. Supervision of actors other than the operator, e.g. suppliers. A **new duty to notify certain procedures** (4 kap. 1 a §) applies from **1 Jan 2027** with sanction fees. Four länsstyrelser get extended supervisory powers [S].
- **Beredskapsplikt / försörjningsplikt for business:** Inquiries only (SOU 2025:6 on total-defence-important activities; national adviser for private-public cooperation reports by **30 Nov 2026**). There is currently **no peacetime legal duty** for private companies [S].
- **Opportunity (strong):** Säkerhetsskydd analyses, säkerhetsskyddsavtal support, personnel vetting support, protective-security consultancy (Combitech, Sweco, Nordic Defence & Security consultancies, Securitas, Svensk Säkerhetsrådgivning). Municipal **continuity planning, exercises, reserve power and water, fuel storage, shelter renovation** (MCF manages about 64,000 shelters [U]). Funding is state-backed and multi-year, so **recurring through 2030**. Constraint: expertise is scarce (cleared staff), which is a barrier for new entrants.

### 2.20 Health and care
- **Socialtjänstlag (2025:400)** has been in force since **1 Jul 2025**, with 285 sections. It replaces SoL 2001:453. Its focus is preventive work and easier access to services without assessment [V]. The planned separate äldreomsorgslag appears **folded into SoL** [S]. A Swedish-language requirement in elder care (prop. 2025/26:93) is proposed from 1 Jul 2026 [S, verify enactment]. Demand: process and IT changes in case-management systems (Lifecare, Treserva, Pulsen Combine), staff training and language testing.
- **Prop. 2025/26:164:** Better control and follow-up of private providers by municipalities and regions [V, title only]. This adds contract follow-up demand.
- **EHDS (EU 2025/327):** In force since 26 Mar 2025. EHR common specifications come about 2027. Cross-border priority data and EHR system certification follow about **2029** (later categories to 2031) [S]. E-hälsomyndigheten reports by **30 Apr 2027** on a Swedish health-data access body system [S]. Demand: regions' EHR vendors and interoperability (openEHR, FHIR) integrators. Mostly **2028+**.

### 2.21 Labour migration (barrier)
From **1 Jun 2026** the salary floor is **90% of the median wage** (SEK 34,470/month from 16 Jun 2026). Some shortage occupations and groups qualify for 75%. Extensions filed by 1 Dec 2026 may keep the 80% floor. Personal assistants and forest berry pickers are excluded [V, Migrationsverket/regeringen]. This is a **barrier** for labour-intensive SMEs (cleaning, restaurants, construction, care). It creates niche demand for immigration advisory but is a net negative for hiring.

### Key takeaways
1. **Recurring public-sector spend is concentrated in cyber (NIS2), civil defence and preparedness, and buildings and energy.** All three have legal force today plus state money, the civil-defence funds especially.
2. **Product-regulation waves (CRA Dec 2027, Machinery Jan 2027, EUDR Dec 2026, PPWR Aug 2026)** create hard-deadline demand among Swedish manufacturers and forest companies in 2026–2027.
3. **ESG reporting (CSRD/CSDDD) and DPP are the main hype zones** for SMEs after Omnibus I. Pay Transparency is delayed indefinitely in Sweden.
4. **Political risk:** The Sept 2026 election and unresolved government formation could delay pending Swedish bills (CER, personalliggare, AI and Data Act supplementary laws, Pay Transparency, the e-invoicing mandate). EU regulations with direct effect (CRA, AI Act, PPWR, EUDR, Machinery, AMLR, DORA, Data Act) are unaffected.
