## 2. Cross-sector anchor: ARN complaint statistics 2025 [S1]

ARN is Sweden's National Board for Consumer Disputes. It received **24,603** cases in 2025, against 25,685 in 2024 and 32,303 in 2023. The number of cases rose in only four departments: **motor, insurance, furniture and laundry**.

| Shoes | 1,189 | 552 | 337 | −39% |

Three qualitative findings from the same report:

- Compliance with ARN decisions was 76% in 2025.
- Low compliance is "especially in the motor department, where it is mainly smaller companies such as smaller car dealers and workshops that do not follow the decisions."
- Foreign sellers, social-media purchases and dropshipping increasingly ignore ARN.

Part of the 2024 drop is explained by the SEK 150 filing fee introduced in August 2024 (summary of the ARN 2024 annual report, via search). From August 2025, ARN no longer reviews disputes under SEK 3,000 [S3].

## 3. Per-sector detail

### 11. Education & professional training (YH, upskilling, compliance training, driving schools)

- **Market size.** In 2025 YH (yrkeshögskolan, Swedish higher vocational education) had about **104,600 applicants**, **77,900 eligible applicants**, **33,400 places** (down from 34,700) and **34,500 programme starters** (−4%). Starters on short YH courses rose **31% to 25,700** [S33]. The largest programme areas were engineering and manufacturing (6,340 starters), economics/administration/sales (6,250), IT (5,680) and construction (5,610) [S33]. National vocational training starters were 1,112 in 2025 against 511 in 2024 [S34]. [GAP] The SEK value of the corporate training market.
- **Buyers.** For YH, the providers are about 2.3 eligible applicants per place [S33]. For driving, the buyers are private learners plus driving schools. The industry body STR says it represents about 75% of driving schools [S38]. [GAP] Total number of driving schools.
- **Regulation.**
  - The cybersecurity law (2025:1506), Sweden's NIS2 implementation, entered into force on **15 Jan 2026** [S35]. MCF's impact assessment covers regulations on security measures *and training* [S35]. Vendor sources claim 18 sectors are covered and that management must be trained (vendor claim, unverified) [S35b].
  - Since August 2026, a passed theory test for a B licence is valid for **one year instead of four months** [S36].
  - Fees were raised because Trafikverket otherwise risked a SEK 201 m deficit in 2025 (search summary of lagen.nu/fee material) [S37b].
- **Pain.** Swedes failed theory or driving tests **376,000 times in 2025** [S37]. Pass rates on the practical test were 48% in 2021 and 46% in 2022 [S37c]. The government has tasked Transportstyrelsen with reducing failed tests [S37c]. A driving-instructor shortage is described as large in a 2022 Skåne report [S38b]. Trafiko was recruiting instructors across 33 cities in August 2026 [S38c].
- **Dominant suppliers.** Large YH providers and driving-school chains such as Trafiko [S38c]. [GAP] Shares.
- **Tech adoption.** Theory-practice apps are common; a consumer guide quotes about SEK 800 for an STR app [S37b]. Driving-school operations themselves are, as far as I can see, poorly digitised [HYP].
- **Recurring revenue.** Medium. Compliance training renews yearly, but driving learners churn by design.
- **Barriers.** Low for software. High for running a YH programme (MYH approval rounds).
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **"Ready-for-test" readiness scoring for driving schools (B2B2C).** This targets the 376k failed attempts. Price is about SEK 1,000–1,400 per practical-test attempt (commercial guide [S37b]; verify against Trafikverket), and the government wants fewer failures [S37c]. Sell to schools as a lesson-logging and readiness tool that cuts wasted test bookings.
  2. **Management cybersecurity training plus evidence pack for entities covered by the cybersecurity law.** The law came into force on 15 Jan 2026, and MCF regulations cover training [S35]. The deliverable is an auditable training record, not generic e-learning [HYP: verify the exact legal wording].
  3. **YH short-course operations kit.** Course starts grew 31% [S33]. The tool would handle applications, eligibility checks, attendance and MYH reporting for small providers [HYP].
- **Score: 6.** There are real forcing functions (the cyber law, the failure rate), but EdTech is crowded and the public money in YH is gated by MYH.

### 12. Agriculture & food production

- **Market size.** [GAP] Agricultural production value in SEK (search budget ran out). Farm count was **55,400 in 2024**, down from 58,753 in 2021, and is down **40% since 1990** [S55]. The decline is concentrated in mid-sized farms [S55]. Holdings with cattle went from 15,227 (2021) to 14,091 (2024) [S55].
- **Failures.** Bankruptcies in agriculture, forestry and fishing rose **12% in 2025** [S2].
- **Margins.** Perceived profitability was high in 2025 for crop growers and diverged for livestock producers (Lantbruksbarometern and LRF Konsult, via search) [S57]. These are self-assessments, not register data.
- **Pain.**
  - An SLU study found about **2.7 hours per week** of authority-related administration, ranging from 5 minutes to 16 hours. That annualises to about 140 h per year [EST] [S56].
  - Subsidy applications cost about **SEK 34,000 per firm** over the study's period (SLU) [S56].
  - Some county boards take up to **60 hours of handling time per investment-support application** (Riksdag question, date unclear) [S56b].
  - Swedbank markets "collect the farm's data in one place and save time at inspections" [S56c], which signals inspection-preparation pain.
- **Acquisition channels.** LRF, Hushållningssällskapet, advisory firms and banks (Swedbank). Farmers buy on trust and seasonally.
- **Regulation.** CAP / SAM applications (the annual EU farm-support application), animal-welfare inspections and environmental rules.
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Inspection-ready digital farm binder** for livestock farms: animal welfare, medication, manure and cross-compliance records [S56][S56c]. Sell through advisers or banks.
  2. **Subsidy-application co-pilot for advisers.** This attacks the SEK 34k admin cost per firm [S56], not the farmer directly.
- **Score: 4.** The pain is real, but the buyer base is shrinking, ARPU is low, sales cycles are seasonal, and Jordbruksverket's own e-services already compete.

### 13. Retail & e-commerce (returns, Temu/Shein, store closures)

- **Market size.** Swedish e-commerce reached **SEK 153 bn in 2025 (+10%)**, a record above 2021's SEK 146 bn. Pharmacies and home furnishings grew 18% [S50][S51]. Svensk Handel separately estimated 2025 e-commerce at SEK 166 bn on a different definition [S51b].
- **Foreign platforms.** Swedes are estimated to have bought **at least SEK 6 bn from Temu** "in just over two years". Almost **1 in 4 consumers** have bought from Temu; almost half bought from a foreign site in the past year; Temu climbed to **#7** on the favourites list while Shein dropped off it [S50]. EU customs duty on parcels under €150 is coming [S50].
- **Store closures and failures.** Retail had a bankruptcy rate of 1.6% of firms in 2025 [S2]. Creditsafe reports 641 retail bankruptcies (2025 vs 528 in 2026; period likely January–July) [S54]. Svensk Handel flags **3,100 clothing and shoe stores** at risk of bankruptcy or reconstruction (July 2026, a risk estimate) [S51c]. [GAP] A verified count of store closures in 2025.
- **Customer dissatisfaction.** ARN textile and shoe complaints collapsed (−49% and −39%) [S1]. That more likely reflects the fee and the SEK 3,000 floor than happier customers [EST interpretation]. ARN names dropshipping and foreign sellers as a compliance problem [S1]. 85% of consumers want clear return-procedure information (E-barometern 2025, figure from the report's info table) [S50].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Returns and complaints-handling back office for small Swedish e-retailers.** The tool would cover ARN-ready dispute documentation and the right-of-withdrawal workflow [S1][S50]. [HYP] Willingness to pay.
  2. **Producer-responsibility (packaging) registration and reporting for micro e-retailers.** This is tied to the packaging reform [S14] [HYP: verify the registration burden on small producers].
- **Score: 5.** Huge fragmentation, but the buyers are margin-squeezed and plenty of incumbent SaaS exists.

### 14. Hospitality & tourism

- **Market size.** Restaurant sales volume through Q3 2025 was **unchanged vs 2024** (Visita, Restaurangvanor 2025) [S52]. Consumer net intention to spend on restaurants was −13, less negative than the year before [S52]. Summer 2025 had **30.8 m commercial guest nights (a record, +3.4%)** [S53]. Regionally, Gothenburg passed 6 m guest nights (+7%) and Östergötland had 2.14 m (+5.2%) [S53b]. [GAP] National turnover.
- **Failures.** Hotels and restaurants were the **most bankruptcy-prone sector in 2025, with 2.8% of firms bankrupt** [S2]. Creditsafe counts **566** hotel and restaurant bankruptcies (2025, period likely January–July) against 479 for the same period in 2026 [S54]. Bankruptcies were +22% in 2024 [S54b].
- **Regulation.** The food VAT cut in 2026 makes restaurant meals relatively more expensive than food from shops, and 6 in 10 consumers want the cut extended to restaurants [S52].
- **Staffing.** [GAP] Visita staffing-shortage data was not found. Hospitality is about 4% of the labour market according to the Visita report text [S52].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Distress early-warning and cash-flow cockpit for independent restaurants,** sold through accountants. Rationale: the 2.8% bankruptcy rate [S2] [HYP].
  2. **Supplier-side niche: liquidation and asset recovery for kitchen equipment** from bankrupt venues. Rationale: hundreds of bankruptcies a year [S54] [HYP].
- **Score: 4.** The customers are distressed and churn-prone, and the obvious categories (POS, scheduling) are well served.

### 15. Public procurement & govtech

- **Market size.** Procurement-liable purchases total **SEK 1,009 bn per year (18.4% of GDP)** [S8]. **18,015 tenders were advertised in 2025, worth about SEK 982 bn** (+454 tenders vs 2024) [S9]. Central-government agencies alone procured SEK 214 bn in 2025 [S10]. Appeals (överprövningar) were 3,643 in 2025 (−2.5%) [S9].
- **Buyers.** The municipal sector advertises **almost 7 of 10 tenders**. **Over half of contracting organisations run only 1–5 tenders a year.** The average tender value is SEK 53.1 m, and over half of tenders are SEK 1–10 m [S8].
- **Competition.** Bids per tender were **4.6 in 2024 (5.9 in 2023)**. The mode is 2 bids and the median 3. **About 20% of tenders receive only one bid.** Transport tenders averaged 3.3 bids; health and social care 9.6 [S8]. The most common supplier to the public sector is a limited company with **fewer than 50 employees** [S8].
- **Municipal IT.** SKR (Sweden's association of municipalities and regions) states that the case-management system market is dominated by a few suppliers, which creates lock-in and high costs, and that switching is hard [S11]. [GAP] Total municipal IT spend.
- **Procurement behaviour.** LOU (the Public Procurement Act) applies in about 9 of 10 tenders. Almost half end in framework agreements [S8]. Adda (SKR's central purchasing body) frameworks are a fast route in [S11].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Tender-readiness and bid co-pilot for micro suppliers in thin markets** (transport, technical services) [S8]. Tendium and others exist, so the differentiation would be the vertical.
  2. **Contract-follow-up (avtalsuppföljning) tool for small municipalities** that run 1–5 tenders a year [S8] [HYP]. It can be sold below the direct-award threshold.
  3. **Municipal waste-reform operations** (see sector 16). This is the best govtech wedge because there is a hard deadline.
- **Score: 6.** Large spend and slow sales cycles. The small-municipality long tail is reachable through direct awards.

### 16. Waste management & recycling (packaging, textiles, food waste)

- **Market size.** Household waste was **about 4.5 Mt in 2025 (424 kg per person, −0.3%)**. Food waste collected was **44 kg per person (+2%)**, residual waste 137 kg per person (−4%) and bulky waste 152 kg per person [S12]. **Textile waste collected was 26,740 t, +60% vs 2024**, the first year of mandatory separate collection [S12].
- **Regulation (the forcing function).**
  - Municipalities have been responsible for household packaging collection since 1 Jan 2024. Collection in squares and parks is required from 2026. **Kerbside (fastighetsnära) collection is mandatory in all municipalities by 1 Jan 2027**; "easily accessible collection points" are allowed only until the end of 2026 [S14].
  - Compensation is paid via Kammarkollegiet, with the 2026 payout expected around week 30. Naturvårdsverket has proposed a revised compensation model (reviewed 22 June 2026), and from 2029 compensation would no longer differ by material [S14].
  - Textile separate collection has been required since 1 Jan 2025, with exemptions from 1 Oct 2025 for socks, underwear and mouldy textiles [S15].
- **Pain.**
  - Cost coverage for kerbside collection is described as a "big challenge" (Nacka) [S14b], and system costs "could not be verified" (Göteborg) [S14c].
  - Municipalities are raising fees: Partille's 2026 tariff cites the packaging requirements [S16].
  - **4 in 10 municipalities** said they would offer textile collection **only at recycling centres** (SVT survey) [S15b].
  - Avfall Sverige notes that larger property owners contract textile collectors themselves [S15c].
- **Buyers.** 290 municipalities, usually through municipal waste companies or associations such as Sysav and Kretslopp Sydost [S14d]. Also property owners and BRFs.
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Packaging-compensation claim and data-reporting tool for municipal waste organisations.** It would turn bin-registry and weight data into the compensation reporting Naturvårdsverket/Kammarkollegiet require [S14]. There is a hard deadline cycle every year.
  2. **Kerbside-rollout operations software** (bin registry, deviation and missed-pickup handling, resident communication) for small and mid-size municipalities before and after 1 Jan 2027 [S14][S16] [HYP: incumbent fleet-system coverage].
  3. **Textile take-back service for property owners and BRFs,** given that 40% of municipalities only collect at recycling centres [S15b][S15c].
- **Score: 7.** Strongest regulatory pull in this set, with a countable buyer list and recurring revenue. The risk is long public sales cycles; mitigate with direct awards and pilots with waste companies.

### 17. Automotive & vehicle services (EV transition, workshops, battery, charging)

- **Market.** The EV share of new cars was **35.9% Jan–Nov 2025**, and the plug-in share 62.7% [S20]. Mobility Sweden cut its 2025 EV forecast from 40% to 36% [S20]. The 2024 full year was 58.4% plug-in and 35.0% EV [S20]. There were 587,000 plug-in vehicles at end-2023 (MRF) [S22].
- **Dissatisfaction.** **ARN motor complaints were 6,664 in 2025, the largest department and up about 20%**. Small dealers and workshops are named as the main non-compliers with ARN decisions [S1].
- **Regulation.** Since **1 Nov 2025**, used EVs fall under a goods declaration in which **battery health can be shown on a 1–100 scale, with the method stated** [S21]. SERMI (EU access scheme for workshops doing security-related repairs) was implemented first in Sweden [S22].
- **Battery service.** Dekra sells a 15-minute independent battery test [S21b]. **Bilia chose Dekra's digital battery tests in January 2026** [S21c]. Bilweb (July 2026) says battery certificates are becoming more important [S21d].
- **Charging and BRFs.** **8,510 of 33,865 BRFs (25.1%)** have charging funded via the Ladda bilen grant. The pace fell from **2,061 new BRFs in 2022 to 742 in 2024 (−64%)**, averaging 1,037 per year over 2023–2025. About **18,600 BRFs with parking lack charging**, and "**1.1 m households are waiting for a board decision**" (Chargenode, a vendor) [S23]. The grant covers 50% of investment (Nacka Energi) [S23b].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **BRF charging "decision-to-install" service:** board decision pack, quotes, grant application and load management [S23]. The vendor source is biased, but the Naturvårdsverket register underlies it.
  2. **Battery-health certificate workflow for small used-car dealers,** who need it for the new goods declaration [S21]. It can bundle third-party SoH tests and attach them to listings.
  3. **ARN and complaint-compliance kit for small workshops and dealers** (documentation, right-of-withdrawal, dispute handling) [S1] [HYP].
- **Score: 7.** Clear regulatory hooks and a documented dissatisfaction spike. The risk is that OEMs and big chains (Bilia/Dekra) capture battery certification.

### 18. Recruitment & workforce management (staffing, gig, labour migration)

- **Market.** Revenue of the 35 largest staffing, recruitment and outplacement firms was **just over SEK 7 bn in Q4 2024, −17% y/y** (Kompetensföretagen) [S58]. [GAP] 2025 full year.
- **Labour migration.** From **1 June 2026**, work permits require a salary of at least **90% of the median wage: SEK 34,470 per month for applications from 16 June 2026** [S59]. Permit holders can renew without the new requirement only if they apply **between 1 June and 1 Dec 2026**. **From 2 Dec 2026 the requirement applies to them too** [S60]. Some occupations are exempted at a lower requirement (27 occupations per a consultancy blog; unverified) [S61]. Forest berry-picking is excluded [S60].
- **Gig.** [GAP] Status of Sweden's implementation of the EU platform-work directive was not verified.
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Work-permit renewal compliance for SME employers** (cleaning, restaurants, agriculture, care) facing the 2 Dec 2026 cliff [S60]. The window is time-boxed, so this is better framed as a feature of an HR/payroll product than as a standalone company.
  2. **Seasonal agricultural-worker compliance** after the berry-picker exclusion [S60] [HYP].
- **Score: 4.** The market is contracting, the policy is moving, and the window is short.

### 19. Insurance & risk management

- **Market.** Non-life premiums by quarter in 2025 were about 41 bn (Q1, +7%), about 29 bn (Q2, +7.4%), 22 bn (Q3) and 27 bn (Q4). The sum of about **SEK 119 bn** is my arithmetic [EST] [S48]. The rolling 12-month figure in Q3 was about SEK 116 bn (+6%) [S48].
- **Dissatisfaction.** **ARN insurance complaints rose from 1,475 to 1,844 (+25%)** in 2025 [S1].
- **Fraud.** **14,182 fraud investigations in 2025 (+27%)**, and nearly SEK 1 bn stopped. Fraud is estimated at **5–10% of claims, SEK 4.4–8.8 bn a year**; motor is the costliest category [S49].
- **Pet insurance.** About 951,000 dogs and 1,582,000 cats (Agria survey, April 2026) [S29]. Premiums described as rising "extraordinarily" (SVT) [S29b].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Claims-evidence capture for SME policyholders and brokers** (property and vehicle damage documentation) to cut disputes. The motivation is the +25% in ARN insurance complaints [S1] [HYP].
  2. **Fraud-signal data services for small and mid-size insurers and MGAs** [S49]. Regulated buyers mean long cycles.
- **Score: 5.** A concentrated buyer set makes entry slow. A broker-side tool is more reachable.

### 21. Consumer services & household needs (RUT, home services, pet care, elder household help)

- **Market.** **RUT deductions paid were SEK 8.9 bn in 2025, +8.06% vs 2024.** Two-thirds (66.35%) of the RUT amount is home cleaning [S28]. Buyers numbered about **1.32 m in 2025** (read from a chart, uncertain) [EST, S28]. 56% of buyers are women and **41% are over 65** [S28]. Dagens PS gives SEK 8.2 bn for 2024 [S28b]. The cap is SEK 75,000 per person per year [S28].
- **Pets.** 951k dogs and 1.58m cats. 92% of dogs are insured per one Agria survey [S29].
- **Pain and dissatisfaction.** [GAP] No home-services-specific complaint data. ARN does not have a home-services department; such cases fall under General or Housing [S1].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **"Senior household help" RUT bundle marketplace or operator** (cleaning, laundry, garden, IT help, snow) aimed at the 41% of buyers aged 65+ [S28]. The ordering is often done by adult children [HYP].
  2. **Back-office for micro RUT firms** (RUT claim filing with Skatteverket, scheduling, staff permits) [S28]. Competition from existing field-service SaaS is likely [HYP].
- **Score: 6.** Subsidised demand, with growing older buyers. Service delivery is low-margin and labour-heavy.

### 22. Professional services for SMEs

- **Market.** Srf konsulterna has **7,000 members**, about 3,600 of them authorised accounting or payroll consultants, serving **about 330,000 companies**. About **70,000 new firms start each year**, and most want external help [S30]. Authorised accounting consultants went from 2,663 to 2,716, payroll consultants from 776 to 806 [S30b].
- **Margins.** Accounting bureaus' **EBITA fell from 10.6% to 9.2%** (Oxceed, 226 bureaus, July 2026) [S31].
- **Structure.** Consolidation continues, with AI being implemented at bureaus (Spiris industry report 2025) [S32]. Bureaus see the shift from executor to adviser as the main change (Wolters Kluwer) [S32b].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Client-document chasing and month-end close co-pilot sold to small bureaus.** Margins are being squeezed [S31] and staff are short [S30].
  2. **Packaged compliance services for micro-firms** (AML/KYC for bureaus' own clients, VSME sustainability data requests from customers) [S62] [HYP].
- **Score: 6.** The bureau channel gives leverage to about 330k SMEs. Big software platforms such as Fortnox, Visma/Spiris and Wolters Kluwer are the threat.

### 23. Real estate & housing (rentals, BRF, brokers, proptech)

- **Market.**
  - **33,865 registered BRFs** (Chargenode, cross-validated against external sources) [S23].
  - **7,465 registered estate agents at the 2025 year-end** (555 registered, 621 deregistered) [S27].
  - 2026 rents rose about **3.4% on average** (5.1% in 2024, 4.8% in 2025) [S26].
- **BRF stress.**
  - **Over 9 in 10 BRFs raised fees** in 2022–2024 (SBAB) [S24].
  - SBC (about 3,900 BRFs) reports fees **+5.32% for Jan 2024–Jan 2025** and **+3.87% for 2025–2026** [S25].
  - About **14% of SBC-affiliated BRFs dealt with debt-collection cases each month** in 2025–Q1 2026 [S25].
  - Fee increases were the top concern for 50% of members [S25].
- **Dissatisfaction.** ARN housing cases numbered 3,057 in 2025 [S1]. [GAP] FMI 2025 complaint count (a secondary source's table is ambiguous) [S27b].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Arrears and debt-collection workflow for small self-managed BRFs** (reminders, payment plans, member communication) [S25].
  2. **BRF board "capital-plan" tool** that links the maintenance plan, fee scenarios and the charging/climate investments in sectors 17 and the climate-adaptation section [S23][S24][S46] [HYP].
- **Score: 6.** 34k volunteer-run buyers with real financial stress. Large managers (SBC, Riksbyggen, HSB) bundle software, so the target is self-managed BRFs.

### 24. Sustainability & environmental services

- **Regulation.** The government's Council-on-Legislation referral (Oct 2026) implementing Omnibus I **drastically reduces CSRD scope**. The turnover threshold is raised to **SEK 5 bn**, listed SMEs are exempt, and companies with **under 1,000 employees need not provide more than the voluntary VSME standard** in the value chain (Realtid, 27 Oct 2026) [S62].
- **Implication.** Demand for CSRD consulting shrinks. Demand shifts to (a) simple VSME-level supplier data and (b) EPR (extended producer responsibility) compliance for packaging and textiles [S14][S15].
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **VSME supplier-response service for SMEs** that receive data requests from large customers [S62] [HYP].
  2. **EPR reporting for small producers** (packaging, and textiles from EU EPR) [S14][S15] [HYP].
- **Score: 4.** The regulatory tide went out. Best treated as a feature of the waste or SME-services plays.

### Funeral / death-care services

- **Volume.** **92,200 deaths in 2025**, 944 more than in 2024 (SCB, 24 Feb 2026) [S40].
- **Dominant supplier.** The Fonus co-operative's funeral business had **revenue of SEK 1,244 m in 2025** and **242 funeral offices**, and reached its **"highest market share ever" according to SCB statistics** (percentage not stated) [S41]. Its legal arm (Familjens Jurist) had **SEK 444 m revenue** and 21 offices [S41]. Fonus offers 25% off digital services to members [S41].
- **Regulation and competition.**
  - The Swedish Competition Authority (KKV, 8 Feb 2024) asked the government to investigate regulation of **church-run funeral bureaus**, which have competitive advantages, and found supervision lacking [S42].
  - A Riksdag interpellation (2024/25:247), citing SVT, describes bureaus **embezzling money from estates** and proposes entry requirements [S43].
  - Complaints go to Konsumentverket or SBF's own complaints board [S43b].
  - No licence is needed to run a bureau (secondary source, low reliability) [S44].
- [GAP] Average funeral cost, number of bureaus, and Konsumentverket price-transparency findings.
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Estate administration (dödsbo) workflow for bereaved families**: estate inventory (bouppteckning) preparation, account closures and subscriptions. Fonus's SEK 444 m legal arm shows demand [S41], and there are about 92k deaths a year [S40].
  2. **Operating system for independent funeral bureaus,** including published price lists and estate-handling controls, positioned as a trust signal against rogue operators [S43][S42] [HYP].
- **Score: 5.** Steady, non-cyclical demand and a trust gap, but a sensitive channel and a strong incumbent.

### Climate adaptation services

- **Evidence.**
  - **About 165,000 natural-hazard claims totalling almost SEK 10 bn over 2015–2024**, with storms and floods most common [S45].
  - SEK 1.6 bn paid in 2023 [S45b]; about 19,000 claims and about SEK 1.1 bn in 2024 (SGBC) [S45c].
  - **Over 60% of flood claims are sewer backflow, largely preventable with backflow valves** (Svensk Försäkring, consultation response on SOU 2025:51) [S46].
  - Some insurers already **refuse cover in flood-prone areas** [S46].
  - **Only 2 in 5 homeowners trust their municipality's extreme-weather measures** [S47].
  - Svenskt Vatten's survey shows many municipalities are poorly prepared for cloudbursts (via SVT) [S46b].
- [GAP] Status of a climate-adaptation bill following SOU 2025:51.
- **Candidate opportunities**
  1. **Backflow-protection retrofit programme for villas and BRFs,** with insurer-ready documentation (survey, valve install, certificate) [S46].
  2. **Cloudburst risk screening and adaptation plan for BRFs and small property owners,** feeding the capital-plan tool in sector 23 [S45][S46].
- **Score: 5.** Strong logic and rising salience. The buyer is reactive (buys after damage), and municipal VA (water and sewage) responsibility muddies who pays.
