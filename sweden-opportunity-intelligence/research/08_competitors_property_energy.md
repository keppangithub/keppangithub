### Market structure
**Fragmented, with a gap no one serves well.** Communication apps (Boappa), maintenance-plan engines (Planima, AFRY), and full-service managers (SBC, Nabo, HSB, Riksbyggen) each cover part of the need. None of the sources reviewed offer a **cross-domain statutory compliance calendar** (OVK, energideklaration, SBA, hiss, radon, laddinfra) for self-managed boards at app prices. The new 1 Oct 2026 energideklaration rules, the EPBD and Boverket's finding on weak OVK oversight (below) all add compliance load.

### Spend evidence and price benchmarks
- BRFs already pay **3 900–13 900 kr/yr for a board app** (Boappa) and **~18 000 kr/yr (1 500 kr/mo) for consultant-backed digital underhållsplan** (AFRY) [S7][S12].
- Ekonomisk förvaltning costs **200–1 000 kr/lgh/yr** depending on scope (Offerta) [S13].
- Boappa's 2 000+ payers on ~9 kr/lgh/mo show willingness to pay for software, with very low churn [S5].

## 2. Bundled building-inspection subscription (OVK + energideklaration + EPBD inspections + radon)

### Regulatory facts
- **OVK oversight is weak.** Boverket's PBL follow-up for 2025 (pub. Apr 2026) found that only 50% of municipalities say they run active OVK supervision. 99% register incoming protocols, 91% review them, and ~80% send reminders. **Only half track which buildings are liable**, which is worse than in 2015. Boverket floats a national OVK register [S20]. **A national share of buildings with approved OVK: not found.**
- OVK interval: FT/FTX ventilation in multi-dwelling buildings every 3 years, schools every 3 years. S/F/FX normally have no recurring OVK under the new rules, but older sources say 6 years, so verify (Kiwa, Jul 2026) [S21].
- **Energideklaration:** new Boverket rules took effect **1 Oct 2026**, with a new summary format, "energiprestandatal" replacing primärenergital, and an A0 class. EPBD law changes took effect 1 Jul 2026 [S22]. Boverket's heating/AC inspection guidance was updated 1 Oct 2026, but the page text does not state thresholds [S23]. **A specific 1 Jul 2026 obligation for HVAC inspections of BRFs could not be confirmed. Verify before modelling.**
- Certification bodies for OVK inspectors: Kiwa, RISE, Svensk Certifiering Norden, Incert [S24]. **Number of certified OVK inspectors and energy experts: not found.**

## 3. BRF EV-charging "decision-to-install" service

### Market data (ChargeNode report, 9 Mar 2026 [S1])
- **8 510 of 33 865 BRFs (25.1%)** received Ladda bilen grants. Penetration among BRFs with parking is ~31% (80% parking assumption). **~18 600 addressable BRFs remain without charging.**
- 141 624 charge points installed, an average of 16.6 per BRF (14–15 in 2024–25). Total grant 1.49 bn kr, **average 175 tkr per BRF**.
- New BRFs per year: 2 061 at the 2022 peak, then 742 in 2024. The 2023–25 average is 1 037/yr, which implies full penetration ~2043.
- Average grant per charge point rose from ~10 200 kr (2020) to ~12 000 kr (2025). Barriers named: AGM decision process, lack of competence in small BRFs, grid capacity, grant uncertainty.

### Grant status 2026
- **Ladda bilen is open year-round for BRFs in 2026**: up to **15 000 kr per charge point, capped at 50% of investment**, new points only. Since 1 Feb 2026, applicants with economic activity must apply before installation. Non-business applicants can apply up to 6 months after installation (Länsstyrelsen Dalarna, 16 Feb 2026 [S33]; Offerta, 25 Sep 2026 [S34]). Klimatklivet 2025 was a record year with 52 000 charge points supported [S35]. Note: Naturvårdsverket pages returned 404 on direct fetch, so these points rest on secondary official sources (Länsstyrelsen).

### Pricing models and benchmarks
- **Capex model:** the grant covers ≤50% (≤15k/point). Average grant ~12k/point implies installed cost of roughly ≥24k kr/point (analyst inference). BRFs recover cost through a fixed member fee: e.g. **150 kr/mo/space with grant vs ~300 without (10-year payback)**, 300 kr/mo, 350 kr/mo, 400 kr/mo in BRF documents and forums [S41] (M/L).
- **Opex / CaaS:** Wattif fixed infrastructure fee plus user box rent. Elaway ~69 kr/mo per user. Some operators pay the owner 25 öre/kWh [S39][S41].
- **Market classification:** **consolidating after a shakeout** (Eways bankruptcy) and maturing ("pioneer phase over"). The remaining ~18.6k BRFs are harder cases: inner-city, old buildings, garages, grid limits. That is where a **decision-pack / project-management service** fits. Hardware and operation are crowded.

## 4. Orphaned solar/battery/heat-pump service and warranty rescue

### Installed base and failures
- ~**314 600 grid-connected PV systems, ~5 541 MW at end-2025**. 21 600 new systems in 2025. More than 100 000 installed in the record year 2023, then roughly halved in 2024 (secondary press citing Energimyndigheten/SCB [S44][S45]) (M; verify in Energimyndigheten PxWeb EN0123).
- **Installer bankruptcies:** 147 (2024) and **196 (2025)** per Laddtorsk. Another count gives 160 in Jan–Oct 2025. 19 bankruptcies in Jan–Feb 2026 vs 39 in the same period of 2025 (search summary of Affärsvärlden/Realtid; paywalled, M) [S46].
- Named failures: **Sesol** (Soltech subsidiary, once ~1 000 employees, ~180 left) filed for bankruptcy on **17 Mar 2026** [S47]; **Energihem i Sverige** (debts ~130 MSEK, thousands of prepaid customers) [S48]; Soldags, Solifokus, El av Sol Sverige (2026, M) [S46]; **Eways** (EV, Dec 2024) [S43]. Svea Solar was investigated by Elsäkerhetsverket for serious incidents. It reports ~20 000 installations since 2019 (undated article) [S49].
- Policy headwinds: green-tech deduction for solar cut from 20% to 15% from 1 Jul 2025, and the micro-production tax reduction removed from 1 Jan 2026 [S45].
- The workmanship (installation) warranty is usually lost on bankruptcy. The manufacturer warranty on panels and inverters survives, but often excludes labour [S50].

## 5. Water-damage prevention retrofit (vattenfelsbrytare / sensors)

### Problem size
- Insurers paid **>3 bn kr for water damage in Swedish households in 2025** (Svensk Försäkring via Dagens PS). Total cost is higher once deductibles and depreciation are included [S55]. Vattenskadecentrum estimates deductibles plus depreciation add another 30–40% [S56].
- Vattenskadecentrum's 2025 report is based on **28 183 water damages in småhus (villas) in 2025**. Kitchen damage rose for the 8th year running [S57]. The older all-housing estimate is >100 000 damages/yr at 6–10 bn kr (Ahlsell 2021, citing VSC) [S58].

## 6. Grid-connection services (DSO case-management software and connection consulting)

### Market facts
- Number of DSOs: about **160 elnätsföretag** own the regional and local networks (Ellevio). Older counts: ~155 (Energiföretagen 2016), 173 (Ei 2006) [S66].
- Transmission queue: **>175 GW of production requests (2025)**, ~7× peak load (secondary wiki, L). Offshore wind applications alone are 125 GW [S67].
- SvK charges an investigation fee for TSO connection: **100 000 kr fixed plus 1 200 kr/MW (2025)**, CPI-indexed (SvK principles 2025) [S68]. This is **direct evidence that connection applicants pay for process**.
- Policy: SvK's government assignment on the connection process (final report 30 Apr 2026) proposes zonal allocation instead of first-come-first-served. Ei proposes that a regulator approve DSO connection terms and fees from 1 Jan 2027, and that the timetable duty in 4 kap. 8 § ellagen be widened to all connections. SvK's recommendations to DSOs on case handling (Jan 2024) are non-binding [S69][S70][S71]. **Binding Ei maximum handling times: not found.**
